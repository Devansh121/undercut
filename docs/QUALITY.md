# Quality scorecard

Scores are assigned by an independent playtester from evidence on the current build, never by the builder of the feature. Score honestly: a 3 with a list of giveaways is worth more than a 4 nobody would agree with.

**Scale:** 1 placeholder · 2 works but looks programmer-made · 3 decent indie game · 4 passes for a commercial game at a glance · 5 holds up under scrutiny.
