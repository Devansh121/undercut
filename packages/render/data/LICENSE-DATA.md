# Dressing data licence

The dressing files in this folder (`*.dressing.json`) are databases derived from OpenStreetMap and
from the Copernicus WorldDEM-30 elevation model, combined with authored placements (grandstand
capacity and facing, boards, marshal posts, gantries and other trackside objects). They are not
covered by the repository's MIT licence.
