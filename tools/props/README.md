# Modular circuit kit

`npm run props:build` runs Blender 5.2.4 from the cached tarball with two CPU threads. No render or GPU is used. All geometry, surface grain and vertex AO are authored in this repository. Run `flock ~/.cache/undercut/gpu.lock npm run props:preview` for inspection sheets and scale captures using the game's Environment, High shadows, sky and afternoon lighting. The preview closes its browser and server on completion.

## Delivery contract

`packages/render/data/props.catalog.json` version 1 is the only catalogue; files are URL paths relative to the web root. No runtime loader or placement code is imposed on the dressing slice. The inspected dressing revision was `08e87417758aa8e534985379874da996fad23ffa`; it has procedural stand/crowd geometry and no catalogue consumer.

- Metres, +Y up, +X front, ground-centre origin, identity mesh transforms. Module runs tile along +Z at `moduleLength`; posts belong to the starting end. Wall joint gaps are intentional. Stand roofs and floor modules meet at their boundaries.
- Three LODs: High to 80 m, Mobile to 250 m, Far to 1200 m. These are suggestions for placement, to be tuned against projected size. Individual figures should be culled earlier or replaced by the dressing crowd atlas at long range.
- Each LOD records shipped triangle count, byte size, file and budget. Limits are 30k/12k/2.5k: the near allowance is for the individual grandstand seats, the middle retains the seating and architecture, the far uses seat bands and drops mesh infill. Small props are far below these ceilings. Complete-kit cap: 4 MB including every level; first-race transfer still needs measurement when the dressing consumer selects and loads these files.
- `bounds` describes the High geometry in delivery coordinates; attachments are in the same local frame. Rows use the dressing stand constants: 0.85 m depth, 0.42 m rise, 0.55 m seat spacing. Row attachment origins are seat surfaces, and figures have ground pivots with boots at y = 0: every seated figure exposes a `seat` attachment at its seat contact (the underside of the thighs and hips, measured at build time: 0.424 m for the crowd, 0.425 m for the close engineer; the hip joint is at 0.50 m). Every LOD sits within 6 mm of it, so a figure does not move on its seat at a LOD switch; the build and `seat.test.mjs` enforce this. Place the figure so this attachment lands on the row's seat surface; this includes the seated pit-wall engineer.
- Board faces use the `board-face` material. The +X face of standalone boards, angled grass-edge hoardings (4 m tiling modules), pit-garage team boards, start-gantry header boards, and bridge banners all carry canonical [0,1] UVs for the dressing atlas, with U increasing toward screen-right when viewed from +X (+Y camera up). `start-light-0` through `start-light-4` remain separate emissive meshes; clone their material before driving each lamp independently.
- Crew anchors include role and facing: garage staff and pit-box mechanics outside the bay, engineers behind the timing desk. Clothing material is `clothing`; AO is `COLOR_0`. Multiply the material or instance colour by team tint without replacing vertex AO. Skin, visor, headset, boots and dark fabric retain separate materials. Crowd clothing uses the same convention. Engineers' caps are `clothing` too, so they take the team tint. On the engineers' `skin`, `COLOR_0` carries the baked AO times the face's tone zones (warmer cheeks, nose and ears, darker lips, shadowed jaw) and a per-figure complexion; it stays a plain multiplier at or below 1.
- A material group is one mesh for instancing. Extract each material mesh as its own registry kind and repeat the placement transform; preserve the five lamp nodes independently. Register every material with the game's Environment and cascaded shadows. Retain AO vertex colours and stock glTF PBR properties.

Existing parkland trees remain owned by the dressing branch. The tall catch fence (3.5 m upright plus 0.65 m overhang) uses real wire geometry rather than alpha cards; its 20 cm near grid avoids transparent sorting while remaining an approximation of catch mesh. The far level drops the infill to suppress shimmer; this needs a moving trackside review after integration.

The geometry and dimensions are scripted; byte-identical exports are not guaranteed for the smooth figure primitives (Draco sizes can vary by a few bytes). The build always measures the shipped output and updates its manifest hashes.

## Overcast and night circuit kits

`npm run props:build -- --kits-only` regenerates the twelve circuit kit props while preserving the previously shipped props. The normal full build includes both sets. Both paths refresh catalogue measurements and manifest hashes. The generated size report is `docs/evidence/m2-models/kit-budget.json`; the unchanged complete-kit ceiling is 4 MB. Rebuild the report after the final export rather than retaining an older transfer estimate.

- `street-block`, `street-fence`: 4 m tiling along Z. Place both at ground height; the fence socket extends through the 1.15 m block cap. `street-fence-end` closes the final post at the end of a run. Mesh spacing is reduced with LOD, matching the existing catch-fence convention.
- `floodlight-mast`: 13 m mast, ballast, service hatch, crossarm, three panel housings with rear cooling fins on High. `lighting-truss`: 4 m suspended box truss with two panels and support anchors. Lamp attachments refer to `floodlight-emitter` and provide a downward direction in delivery coordinates; normalize it when constructing local lights. Emission remains stock glTF PBR; it does not illuminate the road by itself.
- `temporary-grandstand`: 8 m scaffold bay, eight bench rows, stair aisle, guards, sole plates and bracing. Row attachments give count/spacing and seat contact height using the existing crowd contract.
- `marina-office`, `marina-hotel`, `marina-pavilion`: generic 7/9/2-storey backdrops, no real landmarks. Window occupancy is a deterministic geometric mask: lit panes use `window-emissive-mask`, dark panes use glazing. This avoids a new runtime shader or texture fetch. Keep these at backdrop distance.
- `hill-pit-garage`: 6 m bay with the existing garage, board and crew anchors, a deeper stayed canopy, cool cladding and a service roof volume.
- `forest-spruce`, `forest-fir`: 18/15 m trees of irregular needle-bough geometry at every level. Far is the Mobile tree: no cheaper stand-in held the tree's tone at the 250 m swap (measurements in `docs/circuit-model-kits.md`). Foliage is double sided with vertex AO; colour is physical albedo without baked light. Trees should vary in yaw/scale when placed.

`PROP_LIGHT=overcast PROP_ASSEMBLY=forest flock ~/.cache/undercut/gpu.lock npm run props:preview -- street-,floodlight-,lighting-,temporary-,marina-,hill-,forest-` captures sheets (High above Far, four rotations) and an inspection assembly with the game Environment. Set `PROP_LIGHT=night PROP_ASSEMBLY=marina` for night evidence; its preview key represents track floodlighting. Scale frames include the unscaled car. No preview placements enter game world data.

`node tools/props/kit-budget.mjs` after `npm run build` records per-prop triangles/bytes and a conservative High/Mobile transfer reservation in `docs/evidence/m2-models/kit-budget.json`. It includes every new LOD and both sky tiers on top of the existing first race. Actual Spa/Singapore request telemetry awaits the dressing integration.

The forest's LOD gate is `flock ~/.cache/undercut/gpu.lock node tools/props/swap.mjs`: Mobile against Far at 250 m and 300 m in the full scene, overcast / afternoon / night, twelve azimuths, interior tone, region tone and solid-pixel count each within 15% and coverage within the transition gate. It writes `forest-swap.json` (with the files' hashes) and Mobile | Far crops; `forest-swap.test.mjs` checks the record against the shipped files. The preview capture's isolated luma check (each tree against black, High against Far) remains as a material check.
