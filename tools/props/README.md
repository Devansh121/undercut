# Modular circuit kit

`npm run props:build` runs Blender 5.2.4 from the cached tarball with two CPU threads. No render or GPU is used. All geometry, surface grain and vertex AO are authored in this repository. Run `flock ~/.cache/undercut/gpu.lock npm run props:preview` for inspection sheets and scale captures using the game's Environment, High shadows, sky and afternoon lighting. The preview closes its browser and server on completion.

## Delivery contract

`packages/render/data/props.catalog.json` version 1 is the only catalogue; files are URL paths relative to the web root. No runtime loader or placement code is imposed on the dressing slice. The inspected dressing revision was `08e87417758aa8e534985379874da996fad23ffa`; it has procedural stand/crowd geometry and no catalogue consumer.

- Metres, +Y up, +X front, ground-centre origin, identity mesh transforms. Module runs tile along +Z at `moduleLength`; posts belong to the starting end. Wall joint gaps are intentional. Stand roofs and floor modules meet at their boundaries.
- Three LODs: High to 80 m, Mobile to 250 m, Far to 1200 m. These are suggestions for placement, to be tuned against projected size. Individual figures should be culled earlier or replaced by the dressing crowd atlas at long range.
- Each LOD records shipped triangle count, byte size, file and budget. Limits are 30k/12k/2.5k: the near allowance is for the individual grandstand seats, the middle retains the seating and architecture, the far uses seat bands and drops mesh infill. Small props are far below these ceilings. Complete-kit cap: 4 MB including every level; first-race transfer still needs measurement when the dressing consumer selects and loads these files.
- `bounds` describes the High geometry in delivery coordinates; attachments are in the same local frame. Rows use the dressing stand constants: 0.85 m depth, 0.42 m rise, 0.55 m seat spacing. Row attachment origins are seat surfaces, and figures have ground pivots with boots at y = 0: every seated figure exposes a `seat` attachment at its seat contact (the underside of the thighs, measured from the High geometry at build time, about 0.39 to 0.43 m; the hip joint is at 0.50 m). Place the figure so this attachment lands on the row's seat surface; this includes the seated pit-wall engineer.
- Board faces use the `board-face` material. The +X face of standalone boards, angled grass-edge hoardings (4 m tiling modules), pit-garage team boards, start-gantry header boards, and bridge banners all carry canonical [0,1] UVs for the dressing atlas, with U increasing toward screen-right when viewed from +X (+Y camera up). `start-light-0` through `start-light-4` remain separate emissive meshes; clone their material before driving each lamp independently.
- Crew anchors include role and facing: garage staff and pit-box mechanics outside the bay, engineers behind the timing desk. Clothing material is `clothing`; AO is `COLOR_0`. Multiply the material or instance colour by team tint without replacing vertex AO. Skin, visor, headset, boots and dark fabric retain separate materials. Crowd clothing uses the same convention.
- A material group is one mesh for instancing. Extract each material mesh as its own registry kind and repeat the placement transform; preserve the five lamp nodes independently. Register every material with the game's Environment and cascaded shadows. Retain AO vertex colours and stock glTF PBR properties.

Trees remain owned by the dressing branch. The tall catch fence (3.5 m upright plus 0.65 m overhang) uses real wire geometry rather than alpha cards; its 20 cm near grid avoids transparent sorting while remaining an approximation of catch mesh. The far level drops the infill to suppress shimmer; this needs a moving trackside review after integration.

The geometry and dimensions are scripted; byte-identical exports are not guaranteed for the smooth figure primitives (Draco sizes can vary by a few bytes). The build always measures the shipped output and updates its manifest hashes.
